Aristotle at the Lyceum

What this activity covers

Aristotle studied with Plato for twenty years, then founded his own school at the Lyceum in Athens, where he taught while walking the colonnade. Where Plato pointed beyond the world to the forms, Aristotle examined the things of this world: he sorted living beings by their capacities, asked after the causes of each thing, and located good character between two ways of going wrong. In this activity you walk the Lyceum with him and try each of those tools yourself. Follow the objective bar above the game window and answer each question when it appears. At the end you can review your responses, answer discussion questions, take a short quiz, and download a report of your work. You can skip any question you would rather not answer. In course mode, which your instructor may set for you, the last question asks for a written answer.
