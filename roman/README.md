# The Roman World

The region for philosophy under Rome, anchored at Alexandria. Three stops, each carrying one of the schools that shaped the period, with the region map at `roman_world_map.html`.

Hypatia of Alexandria teaches mathematics, astronomy, and Platonism in a city dividing along religious lines, and the activity handles her death in 415 with restraint, as a question about what a teacher owes a divided city rather than as a scene.

Epictetus carries Stoicism, built on the division between what is up to us and what is not.

Sextus Empiricus carries Skepticism, the practice of suspending judgment where arguments balance, and the claim that tranquility follows.

All three descend from the Greek schools the student has already walked, and each says so plainly at its opening.
