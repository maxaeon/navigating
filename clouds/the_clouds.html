The Clouds of Aristophanes

What this activity covers

You are home in Athens, and your jury service has not yet been called. It is the festival of the City Dionysia, and you have a seat at the Theater of Dionysus for a new comedy by Aristophanes called The Clouds, and its target is a philosopher the whole city knows by sight. The play advances with the Continue button, questions appear between the acts, and the evening does not end the way you expect. At the end you can review your responses, answer discussion questions, take a short quiz, and download a report of your work. You can skip any question you would rather not answer. In course mode, which your instructor may set for you, the last question asks for a written answer.
