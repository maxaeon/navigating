The Allegory of the Cave

What this activity covers

It is the month of the sacred ship: Socrates is convicted, the sentence waits on the ship's return from Delos, and the city argues with itself. You walk out to the grove of Akademos northwest of the walls, where a young follower of Socrates named Plato, the note-taker you saw at the trial, will one day found his Academy, and where, in Book 7 of the Republic, he will one day ask his readers to imagine people who have lived their whole lives chained in a cave, watching shadows move across a wall. Today he asks you first, and you play one of the prisoners yourself. Follow the objective bar above the game window and answer each question when it appears. At the end you can review your responses, answer discussion questions, take a short quiz, and download a report of your work. You can skip any question you would rather not answer. In course mode, which your instructor may set for you, the last question asks for a written answer.
