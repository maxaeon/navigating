The Monastery

About this page

An abbey in Normandy, in winter, and a unit on arguments about God. The traveler arrives seeking shelter and finds a house full of people who argue about the highest things as a daily discipline. Each stop below is an activity, and the route runs from the scriptorium outward. The stops jump across centuries on purpose: the unit is organized by its question, not by its dates, and each activity states its real time and place.
