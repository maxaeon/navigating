The Rescue and the Promise

What this activity covers

A river ford on the road east, late in the day, past Baghdad. This is a situation on the road. You gave your word this morning to be somewhere by sundown, and something at the ford is about to make the promise expensive. Press Continue to move through the scene, and answer each question when it appears. At the end you can review your responses, answer discussion questions, take a short quiz, and download a report of your work. You can skip any question you would rather not answer. In course mode, which your instructor may set for you, the last question asks for a written answer.

Discussion questions

1. Construct a version of this case where the promise should win. Adjust what was promised, to whom, and what the help would cost, until keeping the promise is the right answer, and say which adjustment did the work.

2. A rule-based view holds that some duties admit no exceptions. A single-principle view holds that one master principle does all the weighing. Say how each would handle the ford, and where Ross differs from both.

3. The near-universal agreement on this case is a fact that needs explaining. Give the best explanation you can that does not treat the rescue as the correct answer, and then say whether your explanation convinces you.
