The Presocratic Philosophers

What this activity covers

Before Socrates, thinkers in the Greek towns of Ionia and southern Italy asked what everything is made of, and whether anything truly changes. You are an Athenian citizen traveling in Ionia, enrolled for the coming year among your city's six thousand jurors, and due home before the courts convene. In this activity you walk a road through their world, test their answers with your own hands, and give an answer of your own at the start and again at the end. Follow the objective bar above the game window and answer each question when it appears. At the end you can review your responses, answer discussion questions, take a short quiz, and download a report of your work. You can skip any question you would rather not answer. In course mode, which your instructor may set for you, the last question asks for a written answer.

Press Begin the journey to start.
