Navigating Philosophy

About this page

This is the world map of Navigating Philosophy, drawn in two views: the west, and the road east. The journey begins with a prologue on what philosophy is, then enters its first region, Ancient Greece. After Greece the road forks, north toward the Monastery or east toward the House of Wisdom, and you decide which way the journey goes. Each region opens its own map, holding one activity or several, and records its completion here. Situations can also happen on the roads between regions, and those are not chosen.
