Ancient Philosophy Activity Map

About this page

The interactive activities for this unit follow the same road that the ideas took: they begin on the Ionian coast with the first philosophers, cross the Aegean to Plato's cave, and end at Aristotle's Lyceum in Athens. Complete each activity to unlock the next. Finishing an activity means reaching its end screen and downloading the reflection report, which you submit in Canvas. Click a location on the map, or use the list below it.
