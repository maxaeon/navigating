What Philosophy Is

What this activity covers

Before the journey begins, this prologue introduces philosophy itself: what kind of questions it asks, and the branches those questions sort into, including metaphysics, epistemology, ethics, logic, and aesthetics. The scenes advance with the Continue button, and questions appear along the way. Your answers are recorded, and at the end you can review them, answer discussion questions, take a short quiz, and download a report of your work. You can skip any question you would rather not answer. In course mode, which your instructor may set for you, the last question asks for a written answer.
