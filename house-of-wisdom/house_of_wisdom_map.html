The House of Wisdom

About this page

Baghdad, and the road that leads to it and out of it. The region covers the movement that carried Greek philosophy and science into Arabic, and the falsafa tradition that grew from it. The route runs from the road in, through the city, to the road out toward India. The stops jump across decades and cities on purpose. The unit is organized by its question, what happens to philosophy when it changes languages and lands, and each activity states its real time and place.
