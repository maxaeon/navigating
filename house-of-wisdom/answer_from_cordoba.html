The Answer from Cordoba

What this activity covers

Cordoba, in al-Andalus, around 1180. The chief judge of the city spends his early mornings on a project commissioned by his ruler, commentaries on every work of Aristotle, and he has also written the reply to al-Ghazali. The activity closes the region’s argument about causes and follows the commentaries north, where they changed how Europe reads. Press Continue to move through the scene, and answer each question when it appears. At the end you can review your responses, answer discussion questions, take a short quiz, and download a report of your work. You can skip any question you would rather not answer. In course mode, which your instructor may set for you, the last question asks for a written answer.
